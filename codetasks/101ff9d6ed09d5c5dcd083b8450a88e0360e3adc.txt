Update the billing cancellation workflow to be more Stripe-aware and observable.

### Goal
Enhance `SubscriptionCancellationJob` so that it:
1. Retrieves the Stripe subscription with enough data to safely evaluate the most recent invoice’s billing context.
2. Cancels only when the subscription is in the specific delinquent state the job is meant to handle **and** the latest invoice indicates the delinquency came from normal subscription lifecycle billing.
3. Emits structured logs explaining when the job is skipped and what actions it performs.
4. Continues to void any *open* invoices for the subscription, including handling Stripe pagination.

### Required functional behavior
- The job reads `subscriptionId` and `organizationId` from the Quartz job data.
- If the organization cannot be loaded or is currently enabled, the job must skip without calling Stripe cancellation/voiding, and log that it skipped (include the job name and relevant IDs).
- When fetching the subscription from Stripe, request expansion of the latest invoice so that `LatestInvoice.BillingReason` is reliably available.
- Proceed with cancellation only when:
  - the subscription is unpaid (use the Stripe SDK’s subscription status representation rather than raw string comparisons), and
  - the latest invoice billing reason corresponds to a subscription creation or subscription cycle event.
- When cancellation occurs:
  - cancel the subscription in Stripe,
  - log that the subscription was cancelled,
  - list and void all *open* invoices for that subscription (limit 100 per request), iterating through all pages using `StartingAfter`,
  - log each invoice void action with invoice and subscription identifiers.

### Constants
Add reusable Stripe billing-reason constants in `StripeConstants` (under the Core billing constants area) for the allowed billing reasons used by the cancellation decision logic, and update the job to use those constants instead of hard-coded string literals.

### Non-functional expectations
- Make the updated job resilient to nulls in the Stripe response (subscription and/or latest invoice fields).
- Ensure the new logging compiles with dependency injection (the job constructor will need an injected logger).
